FLIP FLOP MARKET DATA CANONICAL SCHEMA
======================================

Required normalized columns:
- timestamp_utc : timezone-aware UTC timestamp
- contract      : actual NQ futures contract symbol, not NQ1!
- price         : last trade price or one-second close
- bid           : best bid, required for Tier A
- ask           : best ask, required for Tier A
- size          : trade size, required for Tier A/B
- source_file   : immutable preserved raw-file path
- quality_tier  : A, B, or C

QUALITY
-------
Tier A: tick trades + bid/ask + size
Tier B: tick trades + size, no bid/ask
Tier C: one-second contract-specific bars

DO NOT
------
- Do not edit raw provider files.
- Do not merge contracts manually.
- Do not label NQ1! as an actual tradable contract.
- Do not guess timezone or timestamp format.
- Do not run the pull tournament before coverage and rollover validation.
