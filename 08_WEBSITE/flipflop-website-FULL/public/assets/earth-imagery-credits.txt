Earth imagery used by Flip Flop HQ

Night lights: NASA Earth Observatory, Black Marble 2016 (3600 x 1800).
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/
Rendered with selective night-side emission and reduced background brightness.

Day surface: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, Blue Marble.
Reto Stockli / Robert Simmon and supporting NASA teams.
